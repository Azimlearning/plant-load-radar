# Plan — P2b Business case (`/value`)

> Phase P2b of `refdocs/plant-load-radar-PRD.md` §6. Execution doc: `refdocs/execution/2026-10-08-p2b-value.md`. Why now: the user said the end goal is a working prototype and that the business side needs the refinement (ADR D-17).

## Why this exists

The brief asks the team to **"quantify the cost of today's setup, showing working"** (working capital, lost margin, project delay days, in ringgit). The deck gives one example per cost (RM5m excess × 25%; RM300m × 10% ÷ 365) and says "needs Chin Hin's data" for the rest. The prototype shows a decision but not what the status quo costs, so the business case is a slide, not something a judge can inspect. P2b turns it into a page where every input is visible, tagged and replaceable.

## Scope

**In:**
- One page, `/value`: three cost lines (excess-stock carrying cost, margin lost on missed outside orders, late-handover cost of project delay), each as **low / base / high** with the formula and every step of the working shown.
- A small synthetic inputs file (`data/synthetic/value-inputs.json`, manifested) holding the assumed bands, each with why it was chosen and **what Chin Hin data would replace it**.
- A pure calculator `src/core/value.ts` (no LLM, no I/O) reusing `workingCapitalCostPerYearRM`, `dailyDelayRateRM`, `contributionMarginRM`.
- The same honesty mechanism as the board: view-model plus figure registry plus a test that fails on any unexplained number.
- A closing section: what the pilot measures (cash released, margin no longer lost, delay days avoided) and the data to ask Chin Hin for.

**Out (and why):**
- **Any claim of benefit or ROI.** What share of these costs the rule recovers is unknown until the pilot; claiming one would be an invented number. The page says so.
- **Interactive sliders.** They need client code; the three-band table shows the sensitivity at lower cost (rule 8).
- A forecast of anything (D-19/D-20 and CLAUDE.md constraint 5).

## Approach

The inputs are of three kinds, and each step carries its tag: **public** (the 20 to 30% carrying-cost range, low authority), **scenario** (average weekly capacity, variable cost and margin per m³, and the featured project's price, all from the synthetic scenario), and **assumed** (how many weeks of output sit as excess stock, how many weeks a year are short, how much outside volume is lost per short week, how many delay days a year). Assumed bands are chosen to be defensible and visibly wrong-able, never to hit a target total.

## Risks & unknowns

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The page reads as a finding about Chin Hin | Banner and heading say "illustrative"; every assumed step is tagged; the total is labelled "worked example" |
| LAD cost overstated (applies only after the deadline; materials-shortage extension unknown, OQ-16) | Delay line carries a low of 0 days, is labelled an upper bound, and says LAD only starts after the deadline |
| Bands summed as if they were a probability range | Say plainly that low/base/high combine all-low, all-base, all-high |
| Excess-stock line dominating the total hides the point of the rule | Show each line separately and do not lead with the total |
| Hard-coded figures creep in | `check:literals` plus the UI honesty test; inputs live in a manifested file |

## Definition of done

- `/value` is prerendered; every figure on it is in the model's allowed list (test fails when one is planted).
- The data file is in `data/MANIFEST.md` as `synthetic`; the carrying-cost band comes from the public file through the loader.
- Base-case arithmetic is recomputed by hand in the changelog and matches the page.
- Banner first; every assumed input tagged "assumed" with what would replace it; no benefit or ROI claim.
- `npm test`, `lint`, `typecheck`, `build`, `check:literals` pass; page viewed at desktop and phone width.
