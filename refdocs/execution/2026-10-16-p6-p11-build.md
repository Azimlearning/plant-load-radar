## Status and deviations (2026-10-16)

All phases built. Deviations:

- **Planned weeks vs raw weeks** (P5): the rule, decisions and ledger run on capacity with covered build-ahead applied; the chart and "ask" use raw weeks.
- **`/` is now the overview**; the board moved to `/board`. Nav counts are passed to each page as a prop (an async nav component cannot be rendered by the synchronous test renderer).
- **Seeded history** has five entries (three project-first, two outside-first) so the finance view shows the rule serving the outside side when a project has float; the RM246k tile became RM245,808 from three of them.
- **Call-offs** are limited to weeks at least four weeks ahead; with a six-week horizon that is Week 46 only. Visitor call-offs live in the same file or cookie mechanism as the ledger and are merged into the scenario for every page.
- **Outbox** rows carry only edited words; recipients are fixed server-side. Cookie mode caps each store at about 3.5 KB.
- **Model layer:** the official SDK and structured outputs (the claude-api skill) instead of raw fetch; default model Claude Haiku 5.5 (rule 8), with the skill's default of the most capable model deliberately overridden and recorded in ADR D-21. Recordings are local and gitignored. No live run was possible.
- **Photos:** drawn images from `scripts/generate-do-images.mjs` (needs Playwright); the app route serves only the two known ids through the manifest loader.
- **`.env.example`** could not be edited from the cloud session; the variables are in `env_setup.md`.
