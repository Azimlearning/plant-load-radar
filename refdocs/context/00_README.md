## One-paragraph summary

Azim registered as an individual for Kabel's company-challenge hiring programme. After NTT Data withdrew all its projects, he took Chin Hin Group's *Capacity & Demand Intelligence* brief: Chin Hin's plants (AAC, precast, ready-mix) serve both outside contractors and Chin Hin's own projects, and when capacity is tight there is no shared rule for who goes first, while each plant plans demand alone in spreadsheets. His proposal, **Plant Load Radar**, is *one rule, one owner, one board*: serve whichever request has more ringgit at stake (internal cost of delay vs external margin), let the plant scheduler apply it weekly with a 30-minute huddle for exceptions (no escalation), and give the plant one dashboard of committed demand vs capacity. An agentic AI layer (Intake → Matching → Planner → Writer agents, human checkpoint) does the legwork; a deterministic calculator does the maths; people make the call. Rollout is about three months at one plant, measured in cash released, margin recovered and project delay days avoided.
